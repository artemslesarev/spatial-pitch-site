CHAMP

We compose sound for the space it plays in: runway shows, pop-ups, presentations. Sound that moves with the walk, rooms with their own voice, speakers that are part of the set or not visible at all.

01Pop-up

A pop-up set as a point scan. Seven speakers, all built into the set itself.

An exciter under the top. The product stand plays.

02Runway

Twelve speakers around a runway. The dot is one sound, the heels of the first look.

03Surfaces

Exciters turn panels into loudspeakers. Twelve panels, twelve channels, nothing to see.

04Objects

Design speakers, bespoke objects drawn with designers, or exciters hidden in the set.

A slab of stone, lacquer or textile with the drivers hidden behind the face.

05Process

You hear the show before it is built.

Steps

  1. 01ListenCollection, set, venue, music. We measure the room.
  2. 02ScoreComposition and spatial dramaturgy: what moves, where, on which cue.
  3. 03PrevizA binaural preview of the show on headphones, weeks before load-in.
  4. 04BuildSpeakers, bespoke objects and exciters fitted into the set.
  5. 05TuneCalibration on site so every seat hears the intended picture.
  6. 06RunLive operation, plus a binaural recording of the show for social and archive.
